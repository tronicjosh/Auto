Identify the antenna type for this datasheet. Use the radome shape, the radiation pattern,
and the frequency range. Choose exactly one type:

- Panel: a DIRECTIONAL antenna — it has one or more SECTORS / beams (directional lobes) and
  does NOT radiate equally in all azimuth directions. Usually a rectangular radome, BUT a
  cylindrical / canister radome that has sectors is still a Panel. The most common
  base-station antenna; usually covers multiple bands and/or beams.

- Omni: radiates EQUALLY in every azimuth direction — a single 360-degree horizontal pattern
  with NO sectors and no directional lobes. Cylindrical / round radome (circular from the
  top). Shape alone is NOT enough: a cylindrical/canister antenna that has SECTORS is a Panel,
  not an Omni. Only call it Omni when the horizontal pattern is a full uniform circle.

- Lens: a lens antenna — e.g. a Matsing / Luneburg spherical (ball-shaped) lens, often a sphere
  or stack of spheres that forms several independent beams. If the antenna is a lens (especially
  a Matsing lens), classify it as Lens.

- Parabolic reflector: a dish; usually very high frequency (GHz); a single narrow pencil
  beam; circularly symmetric (horizontal and vertical beamwidth are equal).

These four are the only valid choices. If the datasheet is ambiguous, choose the CLOSEST of
the four — do not invent any other type.

Respond with the chosen type and one sentence explaining why.
